# Argumentation, paragraphs, and author responsibility

Use this guide for outlines, logical review, and structural editing. Its procedures are Paper Craft's interpretation of the writing principles mapped in [source-guides.md](source-guides.md), not official venue requirements.

Use the current [scientific review](scientific-review.md) graph and claim IDs across technical, argument and editing passes. Test consequential premises against realistic [alternatives](counterfactual-audit.md) before making prose more persuasive. Revisit an earlier judgment when its premise changes; document the affected dependency and revised conclusion.

## Construct the argument before the table of contents

Identify the research question, the answer the manuscript claims, and the evidence needed to connect them. Trace this chain: problem and scope; what existing approaches establish; unresolved obstacle; proposed insight or design choice; validation; bounded contribution. A section can serve more than one role. Systems experience and measurement papers may establish a new empirical finding rather than introduce a method.

Give claims stable IDs, then record their dependencies and exact manuscript locations. Distinguish a premise from an observation, an inference, a hypothesis, and an established result. For each edge in the argument map, ask what makes the inference valid and what could falsify it. Surface missing assumptions instead of completing the reasoning with invented facts.

Introduction: explain significance, define the problem, state the specific gap, summarize the approach and evidence, and bound the contribution. Related Work: organize by relevant approaches or comparison axes, compare the closest work fairly, and connect each limitation to the current question. The absence of a citation in the draft does not establish the absence of prior work.

Results report observations and conditions. Discussion explains plausible mechanisms, alternatives, implications, and limits. Conclusion answers the original question within the tested scope. Architecture and systems papers may combine these roles in Evaluation; do not impose social-science section names or a fixed order.

## Paragraph-level review

For each paragraph, identify one controlling claim or situation and its reader-facing purpose. Label its sentences as topic, context, definition, evidence, reasoning, qualification, or transition. Verify each sentence serves that purpose. Split competing claims when separation clarifies the logic; move evidence beside the claim it supports. A topic sentence near the beginning is useful, but purposeful exceptions are valid. Neither a fixed sentence count nor a fixed paragraph length is a quality threshold.

Name the relationship between adjacent paragraphs: dependency, contrast, cause, elaboration, chronological step, or limitation. Supply the missing reasoning rather than adding generic transition words. Define terms before relying on them and maintain a glossary for overloaded concepts, abbreviations, workload names, and metric definitions.

For each figure or table, describe its question, comparison conditions, important trend and exception, then its contribution to the argument. Check the source data before discussing a trend. Separate what the visual shows from the author's interpretation; a plotted association is not automatically a causal explanation.

## Editing and ownership

Edit logical defects first, then information order, then language. Precise prose cannot repair missing experiments. Do not suppress a limitation, causal alternative, or unsupported contribution to create a smoother story. Preserve manuscript language unless translation was requested.

The researcher owns the research question, interpretation, and final text. AI-generated claims, citations, and numerical analyses require author verification against original papers or data. Venue AI-disclosure rules come from the exact current official profile, not this methodological guide.
