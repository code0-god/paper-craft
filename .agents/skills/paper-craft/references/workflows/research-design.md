# Research Design

Use for an idea, incomplete study, or a research plan. Read [novelty.md](../core/novelty.md), [evidence.md](../core/evidence.md), and only the relevant domain guide.

Establish the question, system/workload boundaries, consequential problem, supplied observations, and intended contribution type. Ask for missing information only when it determines the outcome; otherwise make labeled assumptions and proceed with a provisional review. Classify each statement as observed, supported inference, hypothesis, planned method, or proposed experiment.

Build a problem/mechanism/challenge/insight chain. If the mechanism is unknown, offer competing hypotheses and discriminating measurements. Compare available closest work by technical axes, documenting retrieval gaps. Avoid diagnosing lack of novelty merely because prior work has not yet been searched.

Specify candidate contributions with their scope and falsifiable design hypotheses. A systems design, operational discovery, measurement finding, or integration capability can be the contribution. Do not invent an algorithmic insight to fit a method-paper template.

For each decisive experiment record the claim tested, baseline and simpler alternative, manipulated variable, controlled variables, workload/platform, metric and measurement method, confounders, expected discriminatory pattern, and criteria for revising the hypothesis. Expected patterns are predictions, not result values. Choose repetitions and uncertainty analysis appropriate to the method; no arbitrary universal quota.

Prioritize inexpensive measurements that distinguish failure mechanisms, followed by fair baseline comparison, end-to-end evaluation, attribution, and scope/limits. Report feasibility or reproducibility constraints and alternatives when a planned test cannot run.

Return **Research Question; Problem and Scope; Research Gap; Failure Mechanism; Challenges; Candidate Insights and Contributions; Hypotheses; Claim–Evidence Matrix; Experiment Plan; Next Steps**. Separate completed evidence from research yet to do. The novelty audit output fields can be used directly when the user asks example A.
